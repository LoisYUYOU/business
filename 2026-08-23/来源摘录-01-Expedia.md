# 来源摘录：Expedia Group AI 重组

- 来源：<https://www.phocuswire.com/expedia-group-cuts-vp-svp-product-technology-ai>
- 页面标题：Expedia Group makes AI-motivated leadership cuts
- 页面日期：August 21, 2026
- 正文核对摘录：Expedia Group let go of eight VPs and SVPs in a reorganization fueled by AI；公司称要让工程和产品团队更贴近品牌和业务；内部备忘录称过去一年 AI radically changed what was possible；data, AI and agentic platforms consolidated under chief data and AI officer Xavi Amatriain；公司还说明变化涉及 checkout、fraud prevention、payments、servicing and AI。
