# 来源摘录：Google 购买 Spirit 数据

- 来源：https://www.phocuswire.com/news/online/google-buy-spirit-airlines-data-10-million-dollars
- 页面标题：Google to buy Spirit Airlines' data for $10M
- 日期：August 18, 2026
- 主体：Google；Spirit Airlines；Mercor

## 正文摘录

> Google will spend $10 million to purchase the data of defunct Spirit Airlines. The tech giant was the highest bidder in an auction conducted for the data rights as part of the ongoing court-supervised liquidation of Spirit, an Aug. 14 court filing shows.

> The purchase includes emails, chats and customer complaint data, as well as data related to Spirit's finance and accounting, operations, website analytics and the Free Spirit loyalty program.

> However, the court filing states that no identifiable personal data will be turned over to Google.

> A Google spokesman said the company will use the data to improve its products and AI models.
