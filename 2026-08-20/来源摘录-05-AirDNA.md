## 正文摘录

> AirDNA has launched Rentalizer Agent, extending its Rentalizer tool from a revenue estimator into a fuller underwriting tool for U.S. short-term rental investments.

> Built on AirDNA's dataset of more than 15 million listings, it researches an address across six areas: property analysis, review sentiment from comparable listings—selected based on similarity rather than proximity—local market and seasonality conditions, applicable regulation and zoning and nearby events affecting demand.

> AirDNA said the tool consolidates work that previously took hours across multiple sources into a single report generated in minutes.
